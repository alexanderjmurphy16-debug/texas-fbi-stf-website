Duty Guidelines

Members must complete at least 30 minutes of active duty each week. Break time does not count toward the quota. Off-duty time does not count.
Use On Duty when actively performing STF duties, Break when temporarily unavailable, and Off Duty when your shift has ended.
This starter portal stores demo activity in the browser. For a real shared multi-member system, connect it to a database/authentication service before deployment.
